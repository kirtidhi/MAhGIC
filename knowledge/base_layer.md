## A2. Global macro snapshot

**Rates**

**What this means for stock picking now.**
1. Cash costs 4–5.5% in all three markets, so a stock must earn more than the 10y yield. Prefer self-funded, net-cash compounders over leveraged or long-duration stories.
2. Energy, refining and memory are at peak earnings. Their low P/Es are traps.
3. The best mispricing is in sold-off quality (AI-fear software and IT services, medtech, Indian large-caps), but only where the negative is curable.

## A7. Framework calibration notes

**Insider test**
- Count founders and their family vehicles and trusts: Trefler's GRATs and trusts; HCL's founder-family promoter companies.
- Do not count corporate parents (e.g., a conglomerate parent holding a listed subsidiary), governments (BDL), or PE sponsors.
- Director on-market buying (COH) is a positive note but does not pass the ≥5% test.

**Banks and insurers: flag, don't score**
- Banks: use P/B vs ROE (CBA ROE 14.0%), NIM trend, 30+/90+ arrears or NPL trend, CET1, and credit vs deposit growth (CD ratio).
- India: strip FCNR(B) inflows before judging growth.
- Insurers: use combined ratio and reserve trends. We are in a soft market, so expect margins to fall.

**No listed peers**
- Compare against the company's own 5–10-year P/E and FCF-yield range and the nearest economic substitutes.
- Require earnings yield ≥ the local 10y yield unless growth above 15% is visible in orders or backlog.

**Curable vs structural negative**
- *Curable:* Cochlear's Nexa transition and cost reshaping are management-fixable, time-bound, and the moat is intact.
- *Structural:* seat-based SaaS once revenue estimates (not just multiples) fall to AI agents.
- *Neither:* refining margin mean-reversion is a cycle working against you, not a curable dip.

**Drawdown above 70%**
- Before trusting it, verify: going-concern language, recent or pending dilution, debt maturities inside 24 months, auditor changes, promoter pledges, and whether revenue (not just price) broke.
- COH's ~75% fall (Jul 2024 to Apr 2026) passed these checks; the business was intact.

**Negative FCF that is justified**
- Requires capex visible in CWIP or construction disclosures, funded from cash or OCF rather than equity raises.
- Prior projects must earn ROIC above cost of capital, with a stated commissioning date within 18 months.

**Low-P/E trap**
- Sectors marked Peak or Late in A4 must be scored on mid-cycle EPS: a 5–10-year average or 2025-style quarters.
